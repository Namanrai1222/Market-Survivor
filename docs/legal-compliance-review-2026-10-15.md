# Market Survivor — India Legal and Regulatory Launch Review

**Review date:** 15 October 2026  
**Market reviewed:** India  
**Scope:** Current Phase 1 build plus the features planned in `docs/spec/`  
**Status:** Product-risk research, not a formal legal opinion

## Executive conclusion

**Do not publish the current build to the general public unchanged.** The present game is much safer than a real-money trading product because it uses free, non-transferable virtual currency, generated prices, no cash-out, no prizes and no brokerage execution. It is likely capable of being structured as a permissible **online social game**, not an online money game.

However, the public build should first close the launch-blocking gaps below. The most important are:

1. Replace real listed-company and index names in the synthetic Story/Free Play world with fictional names, or obtain a written Indian securities-law and IP opinion plus all required permissions. A disclaimer alone does not neutralise SEBI's 4 November 2024 advisory on unauthorised virtual trading/gaming platforms.
2. Add an age gate and launch as **18+** unless a verified-parental-consent flow and child safeguards are implemented.
3. Add Terms of Use, Privacy Notice, grievance officer/contact, complaint workflow, responsible-play controls and clear operator identity.
4. Obtain a determination from the Online Gaming Authority of India that the released game is an online social game, or obtain counsel's written advice on whether and when to apply. Recheck whether the game/category has become subject to mandatory registration.
5. Keep all outcome-affecting currency free, non-purchasable, non-transferable, non-convertible and non-redeemable. Do not add cash prizes, gift cards, tradable items, paid entry, paid coin packs or broker-funded rewards.
6. Do not ship Time Machine until historical data is licensed for simulation/gaming use, at least 30 days old, covered by the required agreement/audit trail, and documented in `DATA_SOURCES.md`.

With those changes and pre-launch counsel sign-off, a public release is reasonably defensible. Without them, foreseeable outcomes include a SEBI/MeitY inquiry, app or website blocking, complaints to the Online Gaming Authority or consumer authorities, an IP/data cease-and-desist, or a privacy/child-safety complaint.

## What exists today

The code currently provides:

- single-player Story and Free Play careers;
- a free starting Treasury of ₹10,00,000 in virtual currency;
- simulated market, limit, stop-loss and stop-loss-market orders;
- synthetic prices generated by the game's factor model, not live exchange data;
- real Indian listed-company symbols and names and real index names, including “Nifty 50”;
- simulated brokerage, STT, exchange/regulator charges, stamp duty and GST;
- holdings, orders, fills, ledger, portfolio value, market halts and fictional 2030 dates;
- short disclaimers on the landing page and game footer;
- anonymous identification through a one-year cookie and server-side career files.

The present build does **not** have:

- real-money deposits, coin purchases, cash-out, transfers, prizes or redemption;
- live or delayed exchange price feeds;
- execution through a broker or exchange;
- subscriptions or payments;
- multiplayer contests or leaderboards;
- an age gate, verified parental consent or parental controls;
- Terms of Use, a Privacy Notice, a grievance officer/contact or complaint workflow;
- a clear operator/legal-entity identity;
- an Online Gaming Authority determination/registration display;
- a complete non-affiliation notice;
- production-grade account deletion/export or privacy-rights workflow.

## Why SEBI is still relevant

### 1. Virtual trading/gaming advisory

SEBI's 4 November 2024 advisory states that some public apps and web platforms offering virtual trading, paper trading or fantasy games **based on listed-company stock-price data** violate the Securities Contracts (Regulation) Act, 1956 and SEBI Act, 1992. It also tells investors not to use unregistered platforms for investment or trading activities.

The current engine does not use exchange price data, which is an important distinction. But the product reproduces trading tickets, named listed securities, a known index, portfolio P&L and market charges. There is no published safe harbour saying that synthetic prices plus a disclaimer automatically fall outside the advisory. The lowest-risk solution is a fictional universe in Story/Free Play.

### 2. Educational price-data rule effective 1 July 2026

SEBI's 8 May 2026 circular now permits market-price data for investor education and awareness only with a **30-day lag**, without monetary incentives, and under an appropriate legal agreement with protections against misuse and an audit trail. It also says an educator must not use price data from the preceding 30 days with a named or coded security to indicate future price, advice or a recommendation.

For Market Survivor this means:

- no live, intraday or under-30-day listed-security data in any educational or game mode;
- no scraping NSE/BSE websites or using a consumer data feed whose licence excludes simulation;
- no real-value reward tied to a data-based simulation;
- no future-price signal, target, rating, buy/sell/hold cue or “likely winner” for a real security;
- a data licence, source register and usage audit trail for Time Machine.

NSE's public website Terms of Use independently prohibit using its website/app data or content for gaming, virtual trading or simulation. A separate commercial licence must expressly cover the intended use.

### 3. Investment advice and research

General education that is widely available is treated differently from personalised investment advice. The risk rises if the game:

- gives a real-security recommendation or price target;
- uses the player's age, income, risk profile or real holdings to tell them what to buy/sell;
- sells a subscription whose value includes recommendations or research;
- claims that scores, strategies or past game results predict real returns;
- publishes security-specific analysis that falls within the Research Analyst regime.

Keep debriefs generic and retrospective inside the fictional world. A disclaimer does not cure a feature that functions as advice.

## Online gaming classification

The Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 defines an online social game as one that does not involve money/other stakes or expected monetary gain, may charge a subscription/access fee that is not a stake, and is offered for entertainment, recreation or skill development. “Other stakes” include money-equivalent or money-convertible virtual credits/coins purchased directly or indirectly.

Market Survivor's current free, closed-loop and valueless coins fit the social-game model substantially better than an online money game. Preserve these hard rules:

- no deposit, paid entry or purchase of Treasury coins;
- no cash, gift card, crypto, merchandise, discount of material value or transferable prize;
- no player-to-player transfer, sale or secondary market;
- no conversion from XP/coins/items to money or external benefits;
- no paid reroll, paid multiplier or paid feature that changes returns or outcomes;
- no advertising that describes virtual gains as earnings or income.

The 2026 Rules also establish classification/determination, notified-category registration, user-safety and grievance obligations. Before public launch:

1. Ask Indian gaming counsel whether to apply proactively for a game-specific determination.
2. Check the Authority's current notified categories and prohibited-game list.
3. Implement a grievance system and escalation notice.
4. Add age verification/gating, session-time tools, parental controls where minors are allowed, reporting tools and responsible-play information proportionate to the game's risk.
5. Confirm production data location and retention meet the Rules.

## Privacy and minors

The current app creates a persistent UUID cookie automatically and stores career/order history without a privacy notice or age check. The target persona includes a 17-year-old.

The DPDP Act prohibits detrimental processing of children's data, tracking/behavioural monitoring of children and targeted advertising to children, and requires verifiable parental consent subject to applicable exemptions. The 2025 Rules use phased commencement, so counsel must confirm the exact obligations in force on the actual launch date; designing to the full standard now avoids a foreseeable rebuild.

Minimum launch controls:

- 18+ age gate, or verified parental consent before any child identifier/gameplay data is stored;
- standalone, plain-language privacy notice before data collection;
- specific purposes, minimal analytics and no sale/share beyond disclosed processors;
- no targeted ads or behavioural profiling for minors;
- India data residency for gaming data where required by the 2026 gaming rules;
- retention/deletion schedule and self-service deletion;
- breach response and user notification process;
- named privacy/grievance contact and processor agreements.

## Consumer, advertising, IP and content risk

- Marketing must not imply guaranteed learning outcomes, trading success, returns or SEBI/exchange approval.
- Clearly label ads and paid features; disclose recurring charges and cancellation. Avoid dark patterns, forced continuity and disguised advertising.
- Do not use logos. A non-affiliation notice helps but is not a licence.
- Real company/index names can create trademark, passing-off and false-association risk in a commercial game. Fictional names are the practical solution.
- Historical headlines should be paraphrased and sourced; do not copy articles or images without permission.
- Factual allegations about real issuers and people must be accurate, sourced and neutrally presented. Keep fictional scandals attached only to clearly fictional entities.

## Suggested product wording

**Persistent short notice**

> Fictional educational simulation. Virtual currency has no monetary value. No trades are executed. This is not investment advice.

**Expanded legal notice**

> Market Survivor is an educational social game. Prices, events and performance in Story and Free Play are generated for a fictional world and do not represent current, historical or predicted market prices. The game does not execute securities transactions and is not a stock broker, investment adviser, research analyst or exchange. Virtual currency cannot be purchased, transferred, redeemed or withdrawn and no real-value prizes are offered. Nothing in the game is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security. For personalised advice, consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser. The operator is not affiliated with or endorsed by SEBI, any stock exchange, index provider or issuer.

This wording reduces confusion but is **not** a substitute for the structural changes above.
